What happens when you press Protect
  1. It writes a small note: file ID, time, a fingerprint (hash) of your file, a random number, and your message.
  2. It signs the note with this computer's private key, like a signature only we can make.
  3. It hides the note in the tiniest bits of the pixels or sound samples (the LSBs), starting at the position you choose.
  4. You tell person B the LSB count and start position separately, so only they know where to look.
