Image & Audio Integrity Verifier

What this app is for Person A wants to send a photo or audio clip to person B. Person B wants to be sure that (1) it really came from A, and (2) nobody changed it along the way. The app hides a small signed note inside the file itself. You can't see or hear it. Person B's copy of the app reads the note back and checks it.

1. Protect

Person A uses this. Pick a photo or audio clip (or record one) and the app hides a signed note in it. You get back a file that looks and sounds the same.

2. Verify

Person B uses this. Upload the received file and A's public key. The app says whether it's genuine or what went wrong.

3. Attack Tests

Our extra feature. It edits a protected file in bad ways (changes pixels, uses the wrong key, and so on) and shows that Verify catches each one.
